# ============================================================================
# 零售抓取场景 Prompt 模板
# 对应 README.md 第 3.4 节
#
# 格式: "The robot arm is performing a task. Use the right hand to pick up
#        <商品名> from <货架位置> to <目标位置>."
# ============================================================================

# === 货架位置模板 ===
# top level of supermarket shelf      - 货架顶层
# middle shelf of convenience store   - 便利店中层货架
# bottom shelf of grocery aisle       - 杂货通道底层货架
# eye-level shelf of retail display   - 零售展示架视线高度层

# === 目标位置模板 ===
# shopping cart                       - 购物车
# plastic basket                      - 塑料购物篮
# conveyor belt at checkout           - 收银台传送带
# paper bag                           - 纸袋

# === 商品类别模板 ===
# 包装食品:
# cereal box                          - 麦片盒
# canned soup                         - 罐装汤
# bag of chips                        - 薯片袋
# box of cookies                      - 饼干盒

# 饮料:
# bottled water                       - 瓶装水
# soda can                            - 汽水罐
# milk carton                         - 牛奶盒
# juice bottle                        - 果汁瓶

# 日用品:
# bottle of shampoo                   - 洗发水瓶
# box of detergent                    - 洗衣粉盒
# roll of paper towels               - 厨房纸巾卷

# 生鲜:
# bag of apples                       - 苹果袋
# head of lettuce                     - 生菜
# bunch of bananas                    - 香蕉

# === 完整 Prompt 示例 ===

# 示例 1: 包装食品从顶层货架到购物车
# "The robot arm is performing a task. Use the right hand to pick up
#  cereal box from top level of supermarket shelf to shopping cart."

# 示例 2: 饮料从中层货架到购物篮
# "The robot arm is performing a task. Use the right hand to pick up
#  bottled water from middle shelf of convenience store aisle to plastic basket."

# 示例 3: 日用品从底层货架到购物车
# "The robot arm is performing a task. Use the right hand to pick up
#  bottle of shampoo from bottom shelf of grocery aisle to shopping cart."

# 示例 4: 罐装食品从视线高度层到购物篮
# "The robot arm is performing a task. Use the right hand to pick up
#  canned soup from eye-level shelf of retail display to plastic basket."
